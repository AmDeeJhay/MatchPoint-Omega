How to play

  1. Pick your player on the first screen. That's who you are in the chat and on court.
  2. Ask Omega for a match, for example "Find me a match this weekend". Omega picks an opponent, court and time, and explains why in the Match tab.
  3. Confirm and book. Ask Omega to book it. Both players confirm in the Match tab, then the booking is made. Bookings are simulated.
  4. Play the match. Press Play it on the court, or Play this match in the Match tab. You can also pick any opponent and stadium in the top bar and press Play match.

Tip: choose your racket, kit, game speed and difficulty in ⚙ Settings.

Phone and tablet

Drag the joystick (bottom left) to move. Tap Hit (bottom right) to serve or swing. Use the joystick direction to aim.

Click on the court once before using the keyboard, so your keys go to the game and not the chat.

  • Serve diagonally into the opponent's service box. You get two tries; missing both is a double fault.
  • Rally: the ball must cross the net and land inside the singles lines. A serve must bounce before it's returned; after that you can volley.
  • You lose the point if your shot goes into the net, lands out, or bounces twice on your side.
  • Scoring: 15, 30, 40, game. At 40-40 it's deuce, then advantage. In Quick format, 40-40 is one deciding point, and the first to 3 games wins.
  • Full set: first to 6 games by two. At 6-6, a tiebreak to 7 points by two.
  • Serve alternates every game. There's a changeover break after odd games, with music. Set the length in Settings.
  • Surfaces: clay is slow with a high bounce, grass is fast and low, hard courts are in between.

Omega, your match coordinator

Omega's MeTTa rules pick a fair opponent (similar level), a court both players can reach, and a time you're both free. If a court gets taken or a player drops out, tell Omega or press Mark court as taken, and it re-plans. It always asks both players before it books.

Play online

Press Online → Create a room and send your friend the code or invite link. They open the page, pick a player, and choose Join room. The host presses Play match.

Predictions

Before a serve, back a player to win the point with play coins. Coins have no cash value.

Offline

Matches against the computer work without internet once the page is open. Omega's chat needs a connection.
